# Tahap 2 Data Policy

## Source classification
### GREEN — Knowledge reuse
Facts, curriculum concepts, terminology, topic coverage and proven domain structures may inform new questions.

### YELLOW — Reference only
Existing questions may be inspected for topic gaps, difficulty calibration and audit lessons, but their wording/structure must not be copied.

### RED — Quarantine
Items with duplicate lineage, generator contamination, answer/explanation leakage, factual uncertainty, broken options, or audit failure are excluded from generation templates.

## Known lessons from prior audits
- Exact uniqueness is insufficient; semantic and structural similarity must also be checked.
- Direct recall, one-step arithmetic and obvious scenarios were previously overrepresented.
- Distractors must be plausible and competitive.
- Visuals must not contain explanatory/revealing text.
- Explanations must match the actual stem and answer.
- Technology content previously contained repeated generator boilerplate; such contamination must not propagate.

## Non-official status
The product must never describe generated practice questions as official KPM/PKSK questions. Public examples are format/reference material only.
